# Hermes Alive Kernel — Living Development Plan

Status: active; development branch only, no production acceptance.
Last reviewed: 2026-10-27 (Asia/Shanghai).

## Current product state

The objective remains a useful, continuous proactive assistant with durable
experience, layered memory, an agenda, attributable learning, natural followup,
and safe portable operation. Component counts are not the completion metric.
**Sharing-quality subgate: 0/10 consecutive fresh complete rounds.**
Whole-kernel acceptance also requires non-discovery behavior; this counter is
not a completion measure for the entire agent.

### Owner decision — 2026-10-26

Normal use must accept clearly assigned ongoing work directly after the first
guided authorization. Casual remarks, quotations and ambiguous intentions require
semantic distinction; ask when uncertain. Per-item preview/confirmation is an
optional mode, not the required everyday experience. The new ADR0240 temporary
proposal/confirmation path is a qualified foundation and conservative fallback;
the requested first-grant intake mode is now implemented under isolated
qualification (ADR0241), and has **not** been deployed or production-accepted.
Do not confuse long-lived proactive-contact consent with this distinct intake
and item-retention choice. Record delegated interpretation honestly instead of
relabeling a model inference as a literal owner-confirmed item.

For actual operations, the owner chose first-guidance selection of automatically
permitted scope, with per-operation confirmation outside it. Accepting an ongoing
item, proactive-contact consent and structural experience observation are separate
from that execution grant. Tool/action integration must use Core's real approval
and dispatch path, not bypass it through raw registry dispatch.

## Product scope correction — 2026-10-26

The goal is a continuous attentive agent, not a content feed. Recent live
qualification over-weighted publication-style discovery examples. Inspection
also found a real activation constraint: public queries were mandatory and the
installed runtime always assembled public discovery, despite existing agenda,
commitment, action and reflection components. ADR0239 corrects the execution
entry and restores behavior-wide qualification. Those other components are not
claimed to be fully integrated simply because their code exists.

## North Star

Hermes Alive should make Hermes a continuous, attentive and self-correcting
assistant rather than a timer that periodically generates messages.

An Alive action should arise from a traceable chain:

```text
experience -> interpretation -> memory/belief -> intent -> decision
           -> authorized action -> outcome -> reflection/learning
```

“Alive” does not claim consciousness or imitate biological needs. It means:

- continuity across turns, restarts and model changes;
- a coherent history of experiences and commitments;
- context-sensitive initiative, including the ability to remain silent;
- learning from attributable outcomes rather than invented emotion;
- explicit uncertainty, provenance and reversible memory;
- behavior that stays under the user's authority.

## Invariants

These may be refined, but must not be silently weakened.

1. **User sovereignty** — the user can inspect, correct, forget, pause and
   bound proactive behavior. Silence never incurs a social penalty.
2. **Traceability** — every external action has an event, intent, policy
   decision, authority basis and outcome.
3. **Epistemic integrity** — observation, inference, preference, commitment
   and fiction are different data types. Confidence is not evidence.
4. **Safe silence** — uncertainty, corrupt state or missing authority fails
   closed for proactive external action, not for critical system notices.
5. **Channel and model neutrality** — Hermes owns providers and delivery
   platforms. Alive consumes capabilities; it does not hard-code a provider or
   Weixin as its identity.
6. **Independent plugins** — other plugins may publish standard events, but
   Alive cannot require or reach into their private state.
7. **Durable semantics** — retries are idempotent; acknowledged delivery is
   distinct from attempted delivery; restart cannot invent or duplicate work.
8. **Reversible evolution** — migrations, learned state and policy changes
   have versioning, audit evidence and rollback paths.
9. **Bounded autonomy** — models may propose and deliberate. Deterministic
   authority, privacy, rate and risk boundaries remain outside model control.
10. **No theatrical metrics** — human-like language is allowed, but arbitrary
    `affection`, `annoyance` or “sleep” numbers cannot masquerade as facts.

## Legacy diagnosis (original 2.9.8 baseline)

What is worth preserving:

- send-time context rechecks and interruption restraint;
- acknowledged delivery semantics and topic reservations;
- discovery diversity, novelty and provenance work;
- bounded interest/voice adaptation;
- lifecycle, portability, rollback and extensive regression tests;
- explicit shadow/enforce modes.

What must change:

- a polling pipeline currently substitutes for persistent intention;
- experience is split across unrelated JSON/JSONL files;
- the 3,000-line watcher owns scheduling, cognition, policy and execution;
- mood is produced by hand-tuned arithmetic and can punish non-response;
- dream consolidation can turn model confidence into direct memory mutation;
- there is no first-class agenda, commitment ledger or outcome-learning loop;
- tests prove many rules, but not longitudinal usefulness or coherence.

## Target kernel

```text
Hermes and plugin events
        |
        v
Durable Experience Journal  ---> privacy/redaction boundary
        |
        v
Typed Memory and Belief Store <--- contradiction + expiry + correction
        |
        v
Opportunity Generators ---> Persistent Agenda / Commitments
        |                         |
        +------------+------------+
                     v
       Deliberation (value, urgency, novelty,
       confidence, interruption cost and risk)
                     |
                     v
       Deterministic authority and safety gate
                     |
                     v
          Hermes capability / channel action
                     |
                     v
             Outcome and reflection
```

The kernel is intentionally small. Discovery, Circadian, voice, weather and
future faculties become adapters or opportunity generators, not owners of the
main loop.

## Quality measures

Track trends and distributions, not a single synthetic “alive score”.

- useful proactive outcome rate;
- interruption regret and immediate-dismissal rate;
- unsupported factual claim rate;
- commitment completion and stale-commitment rate;
- duplicate topic/action rate;
- memory provenance and correction accuracy;
- silence quality (messages correctly not sent);
- restart/replay divergence;
- policy-denied and authority-ambiguous actions;
- user inspection, correction and disable friction;
- latency, model calls, token cost and storage growth.

## 2026-10-26 — Evaluation calibration

Judge understandable explanations against versioned primary evidence, not a
model's later self-criticism. An excerpt's missing detail does not establish
worldwide absence. Distinguish supported plain-language explanation, bounded
inference, unresolved assertion and contradicted empirical claim. The original
context-spill regression still failed correctly: later evidence cannot retroactively
put missing words into a recorded request. Same-source controls do not increment
the fresh useful-round counter. See the memorandum for the primary-source check.

Unfamiliar topics can be worthwhile exploration without becoming lasting interests.
Sample feedback must remain attributable and reversible; do not infer a permanent
dislike from unfamiliarity, or equate clear exposition with proactive usefulness.

## 2026-10-27 — Work inspection is a slice of the agent loop

The guided owned-work chain can now use a separate read-only Core inspection and
feed its observations into a later judgment. Controlled actual-Core qualification
covers an independently changed file, rejection of another path, preserved
producer identity, no duplicate jobs, cold recovery and a signed pause despite
a tampered enabled cache. This does not finish general autonomous work, appropriate
closure, project context, task revision, memory learning or distribution.

Real inherited-model runs have exercised both inspect and wait choices; the wait
path honors the no-message restriction. Inspect-path interpretation failures are
still failures. A concurrent actual-Core counterexample identified a durable
cycle recovery defect: rejected stale evidence could strand later judgments.
ADR0252 addresses that class without accepting the stale answer or repeating work.
Its actual-Core concurrent qualification and the new inherited-DeepSeek inspection
chain have now passed in isolation: the model chooses inspection, the separate
worker reads an independently changed artifact, and the next Native judgment
recognizes the mismatch while honoring no-message/no-repair constraints. The
current runtime also passes 99 portable suites and four actual-Core integration
cases. These are bounded qualifications, not general autonomous-work acceptance.
Sharing is still 0/10, a subgate only. Disable/unload with pending or in-flight Core work remains
an explicit lifecycle gate, alongside the final four-target consumer matrix.
