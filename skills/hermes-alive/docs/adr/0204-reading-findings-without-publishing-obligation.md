# ADR0204 — Reading findings without a publishing obligation

Status: implemented; qualification pending

## Gap

The existing agenda records a model's question/value hypothesis. Read receipts
prove retrieval under that inquiry, not that the question was answered. Source-led
expression intentionally stopped treating the question as the recipient's task,
but lacked an explicit record of what the reading established or left unknown.
The same source could consequently become a thematic article despite supplying
no answer to the originally pursued question.

## Existing call and storage, bounded advisory notes

Opted-in research interpretation asks for reading_assessments alongside ordinary
candidates and abstentions in the same model call. A note links one current inquiry
to answered/partial/unresolved, at most two exact research-excerpt quotations with
tentative interpretations, and up to three open questions. Only passages actually
admitted to that interpretation qualify. Exact quotation is an integrity check,
not proof of interpretation, importance or independent verification.

The pure normalizer retains complete valid notes and records unassessed inquiry
IDs/rejection counts for missing, duplicate, foreign, malformed or unsupported
notes. It does not salvage fragments or fill in an answer. An invalid side note
does not trigger a new model call, discard a valid candidate or terminate the
whole cycle. The encrypted existing opportunity bundle stores the result under
its original source/inquiry/owner/expiry bindings. Metadata counts expose missing
progress without copying private text into event headers.

Expression resolves the exact current completion and sees questions, findings and
remaining uncertainty as advisory context. They are not new source facts, user
preferences, an instruction to narrate research, or an obligatory outline. A
different genuine discovery may still be worthwhile; unanswered research does
not require publishing commentary, but neither is it an absolute admission gate.
No lexical blacklist, compulsory brevity/criticism, action permission, queue,
permanent preference or completed agenda is created.

## Upgrade and recovery

The installed automatic embedding opts in. Before search begins, the existing
durable cycle-mode mechanism freezes whether this cycle uses the new contract.
Already-started historical search cycles without the marker retain their old
semantics and model spend. Direct library callers retain the default old mode.
Planning also binds explicit opt-in; original synthesis method/bundle markers
must agree before notes may be consumed. Injecting new notes into an unmarked
historical bundle is rejected, not retroactively interpreted.

Ordinary candidate structural recovery remains separate: when the new method was
recorded, its advisory side field is removed before the old candidate-envelope
validator. Reopening validates the original inputs/current dependencies and
retained notes without inference. Erasure/withdrawal/foreign-owner scope or changed
inquiry evidence prevents reuse. Missing advisory output remains unknown on reopen.

## Evidence and limits

Initial network-none tests cover parsing and actual journal/agenda/read/planning/
expression composition; actual pinned-Core installed startup delivers the recorded
partial findings into authoring through seven controlled model stages. They do not
establish real intelligence or better taste. Expanded tests exercise invalid and
missing notes without dropping communication, structural correction, exact reopen,
source erasure and old-cycle upgrade without inference. Final canonical full
regression and real new representative tests remain required.

This is a persistent interpretation result under the existing completion, not yet
cross-cycle inquiry retrieval or automatic pursuit of unresolved questions. It does
not prove full external memory integration, sustained agenda/reflection usefulness,
ten fresh clean complete rounds, platform lifecycle or production acceptance.
