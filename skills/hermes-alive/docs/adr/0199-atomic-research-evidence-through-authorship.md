# ADR0199 — Preserve a bounded research evidence group through authorship

Status: implemented; final qualification pending

## Actual failure, not a hypothetical prompt problem

The fresh installed cited-source trial successfully retained the secondary page
and its cited project repository. Read receipts prove both observed, with zero
extract failures. Yet candidate interpretation received pool6/window5: chronological
packing exhausted its24000-character allowance before the original page. The
writer independently received only its selected canonical page. A final model
finding of supported cannot restore evidence never shown to the writer.

A controlled negative reproduction keeps the same completed reads but removes
the new bundle propagation: the unchanged old packer omits the original page
(pool5/window3 in that fixture). This is not an extraction failure, provider
failure, judgment about message length, or evidence that all claims are false.

## Evidence group and historical scope

The installed preparation passes the exact completed original/follow-up read
receipts as a bounded research group. No new store, researcher agent, judge,
provider, queue, search permission or model preference is introduced. Resolve
each receipt against its original encrypted request, current inquiry, owner,
sensitivity, chronology, original URLs and current retained page observations.
At most two read receipts/four extracts are eligible. A search receipt is not
page-reading evidence. The group never turns research rationale into user facts.

A new durable group mode is captured before search. Previously started cycles
without it retain their old parameters and exact replay: even the immediately
preceding cited-read version must not acquire a new planning contract on resume.
Existing source-led-v1/v2 operations preserve their original windows, author
contexts and review hashes. New grouped operations record source-led-v3 and
explicit read-receipt bindings in planning/synthesis history.

## Atomic bounded packing, not a topic or quota patch

Place the entire required group before ordinary recall. Never silently truncate
or drop one related page to make a qualified share. Keep background items in
their original rotating chronological order and admit a bounded contiguous
prefix. Priority pages do not advance the cursor past unseen background items;
normal wraparound remains valid. At least the next background item must fit
when one exists, otherwise report insufficient context rather than starve it.

Grouped synthesis has a64000-character bound covering up to four8192-character
retained extracts plus bounded context/background. Grouped author/review framing
has a120000-character bound, below Core's131072 message admission. This is an
explicit group contract across stages, not an unbounded window or an alteration
of legacy24000/40000 limits. If the required group or framing cannot fit, return
incomplete; do not manufacture a source-reduced success or automatically retry.
Correction paths remain bounded as well; large-group correction coverage still
requires qualification, not an assumption from a normal-path PASS.

The writer gets the actual research group and the selected canonical source,
even when it selects the secondary page. It gets no private research assignment
or generator-defined recipient goal. The final source/value snapshot binds the
same admitted evidence and actual wording, not the generator's proposed plan.
Page scope is collector-reported, not independent verification or full reading;
do not infer authoritative facts from a hostname, hyperlink or model verdict.

A fresh actual trial exposed a missing semantic handoff despite small-source
controlled tests: v3 authoring was source-led, but the comparator's review branch
still chose its generic meaning-only contract and omitted source_context. It
rejected added claims without comparing the available original evidence. Do not
reinterpret that historical rejection as a successful or correctly scoped review.
New grouped operations explicitly bind bounded-group-source-v1 in the encrypted
proposal and interpreter method; author context propagates this exact marker to
the existing source-aware semantic contract. No prompt/schema/judge is added.
Old unmarked v3 retains the original generic contract and result on replay.
Actual installed tests must assert review source_context and prompt/protocol,
not merely return a canned positive review. A correctly scoped review may still
reject the message; supplying evidence does not predetermine approval.

## Privacy, lifetime, evidence and remaining work

Resolve current inquiry/source content before admission, after model waits and
on retained candidate/review recovery. Withdrawing or erasing one group member
invalidates dependent use, rather than quietly keeping the remaining pages.
Historical fairness validation uses authenticated read/source metadata only:
it must not decrypt forgotten research or prevent a new cycle after an explicit
preference change. A historical cursor is not permission to reuse old content.

Tests cover real packing pressure, writer visibility when selecting the secondary
page, priority/background cursor and wraparound, concurrent withdrawal during
final group resolution, source erasure invalidating a
completed candidate, changed explicit context without historical decryption,
foreign/reordered/search/unbound receipts, old-mode recovery without IO, and
existing pending-read/consent/inquiry privacy defenses. The actual Core installed
probe selects the secondary page but requires both originals in author input.
Sources/models/IO in controlled tests are synthetic; use terminal qualification
and fresh actual runs before claiming useful behavior or a clean quality round.

Full sustained agenda, noisy/external memory and Halo/physical erasure,
outcomes/feedback/reflection, novelty/exploration, migration/rollback/platforms,
ten fresh complete clean rounds and production acceptance remain open. No
production update, live send or completed-Alive claim is authorized by this ADR.
