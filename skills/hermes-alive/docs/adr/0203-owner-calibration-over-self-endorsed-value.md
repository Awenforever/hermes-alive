# ADR0203 — Owner calibration over self-endorsed recipient value

Status: investigation; no new runtime quality policy adopted

## Contrary evidence

An owner read the complete original historical unsent discovery sample and
rejected its generic editorial structure, unearned significance, unnecessary
inventory and manufactured criticism. An earlier model review had endorsed it.
A repeat with the same evidence/text again endorsed faithful reporting and a
coherent topic framing. A separate explicit contribution/burden assessment found
the needless inventory and staged dialogue in the source, but still endorsed the
whole on the strength of one concrete technical paragraph.

These are two logical diagnostic calls through inherited Core main/fallback;
the primary timed out on the second and Core used the configured official
fallback. Both are the same model family, not independent semantic validation.
No sends, production changes, profile update or fresh quality round. Personal
sample feedback and model transcripts stay outside the public repository.

A subsequent two-arm synthetic authorship pilot, with/without the complete prior
sample and feedback, did not show a convincing repair. Both retained useful
mechanisms/notices/warnings and abstained on a generic release; adding the example
could lengthen an explanation with a redundant concluding paraphrase. Expected
properties and control prose were withheld, but descriptive case IDs leaked
evaluative hints. Preserve that design failure rather than claiming blinded
generalization. Corrected future IDs are not retroactive fixes to completed calls.

The installed automatic path uses Core's auxiliary facade. Inheriting its model,
auth and fallback does not mean invoking native AIAgent/persona assembly. The
older synthetic native-host boundary proof offers an existing comparison path,
not proof of improved quality. Explore it before adding another memory subsystem
solely for development previews. No private persona/history may be silently
imported; native and auxiliary invocation limits differ. Do not declare persona
the sole cause or trade source substance for simulated human experience.

A three-arm follow-up compared auxiliary plain, auxiliary with a synthetic identity,
and actual native AIAgent with that identity as SOUL. Sources and neutral IDs were
shared and expected labels withheld. All made the same broad choices; no compelling
quality improvement was demonstrated. Native used the configured official fallback
while auxiliary responses used the primary route, so this does not isolate a causal
host effect. It also does not justify blaming the primary model or changing routing.
No native-path switch is adopted. These were synthetic probes, not fresh real
discovery chains or owner satisfaction evidence.

Next investigate reading-intent continuity: distinguish the question an agent
actually pursued, what original reading established and what remains unresolved.
Do not turn an unsuccessful investigation into a publishing obligation or substitute
a source's general editorial thesis for a discovery. Neither impose the research
question as an owner request nor require all messages to narrate the research log.
An alternate genuine discovery may still be worthwhile. Reuse the existing inquiry
agenda and read receipts before introducing another model stage or persistent store.

## Consequences

Factual faithfulness, a coherent theme and topic relevance do not demonstrate
that an unsolicited message deserves this recipient's attention. One useful
paragraph does not establish that every surrounding paragraph is worthwhile.
A source's editorial thesis or staged role-play is not observed public debate.
Source acquisition, actual substance, selection and expression all contribute.

Do not adopt an increasingly elaborate rubric merely because it sounds better.
This trial contradicts the claim that the new rubric repairs the failure.
Do not grow a banned-phrase list, hardcode topic rejection, force brevity or
invent human experience, excitement or humor. Preserve depth when it has substance;
reasonable criticism and material qualifications remain eligible.

## Next evidence required

Use complete attributed examples and explicit feedback as calibration evidence,
not automatic permanent preference facts. An unsent preview review must never be
forged into a delivered-message reply or an observed channel outcome. Keep source,
owner, consent, lifetime, erasure and operation bindings explicit. Reuse existing
memory/context/feedback consumers where their actual contract fits; do not bypass
delivery attribution just to make an example enter a history window.

Investigate whether calibration changes concrete selection and authoring, not
merely whether a judge can repeat the owner's vocabulary. Test unseen subjects
and sources. Predeclare counterexamples: useful detailed mechanism explanations,
source facts worth a short notice, generic thematic padding, and specific requested
migration warnings. These exercise over-rejection as well as false acceptance;
they are diagnostic controls, not a universal taste classifier.

Show complete original generated messages for feedback, not flattering summaries.
No hidden acceptance criterion based on model self-scores. Source fidelity,
usefulness, conversational expression and lifecycle reliability need their own
evidence; none substitutes for ten fresh complete qualified rounds and production
acceptance. The full sustained memory/agenda/intent/outcome/reflection/privacy/
migration objective remains unchanged.
