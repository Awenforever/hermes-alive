# ADR0195 — Source-led discovery authorship, strict execution and other purposes

Status: implemented connection; canonical and real-content qualification pending

## Evidence

An actual search/read/ExpressionRuntime trial completed nine logical model stages;
a tenth call exercised its existing final-source checkpoint without regenerating
the first nine. Source support, plan purpose and atom preservation all passed.
The375-character final message faithfully retained a family introduction,
architecture inventory and an operating-verification caveat. Manual product review
did not accept it as a natural useful contribution. That is not proof of a factual
error or a word-count rule: the renderer correctly preserved the prescribed plan.

The misplaced responsibility is freezing a discretionary share's source notes as
mandatory recipient meaning before the author can choose the contribution. More
judges, phrases to suppress or source/topic exceptions do not fix that boundary.

## Decision

Communication-plan-v3 is an editorial brief for discovery-provenance SHARE with
share/inform speech act only. Its contents must be empty: recipient_goal is a
revisable hypothesis, not facts to repeat, user intent or permission. Requested
reports/admitted commitments, asks, reminders and warnings retain strict v1/v2
meaning. Source scope is checked at admission and again at expression binding.

The actual author receives the selected canonical URL's original retained source
window, collector/read scope, current explicit owner context and bound tentative
reading task. It chooses a coherent contribution and necessary qualifications.
The canonical link and speech act remain required; no source-fact inventory is
made obligatory. Sources, selection, expiry, owner context, privacy, policy and
source withdrawal stay current across awaits and recovery.

Plan-as-message preflight judges are deferred for v3, not reported as supported.
Its actual final message MUST pass the existing final checkpoint, now assessing
source support AND recipient value from actual text. This happens even if a
caller disables ordinary grounding. The semantic review sees original source
context: sourced facts absent from required atoms are not automatically additions.
All verdicts remain model evidence, not world truth or human satisfaction.

Final-review obligation is enforced by SendPreparationRuntime, permit issuance,
cached permit recovery AND lower dispatch validation. Exact owner confirmation,
delegation or an old signature cannot omit that witness. It must concern the
current actual expression; a draft review cannot stand in. No send grant, transport
or physical queue is created by a brief or a favorable review.

## Data and recovery contracts

New synthesis records source-led-v1 authoring capability and proposal-shape-v3
recovery. Historical admission/recovery retains versions1/2, so a previously
invalid v3 response is not retroactively relabeled valid. Meaning/source semantic
recovery records semantic-source-findings-v1 with the exact original source
context and prompt digest. Old meaning-only recovery keeps semantic-findings-v1.
Final review chooses its versioned prompt from a reconstructed bound snapshot,
not an unsigned success label or today's mutable default. Completed operations
reopen without inference; uncertain starts are not retried implicitly.

## Qualification and remaining scope

Controlled encrypted integration covers author choice of a supported fact absent
from required atoms; unused source inventory omitted; semantic protocol recovery
and reopen; one final review instead of plan-as-message judges; forced final gate
when groundingfalse; negative final review; otherwise valid owner confirmation
without witness denied; valid witness admitted; lower dispatch refusal if witness
removed; source erasure during render; strict purpose/provenance admission; old
parser scope and malformed-plan isolation. No real model/channel in these tests.
Existing opportunity/semantic recovery, commitments, preparation and shadow final
quality regressions pass. The first fixture asserted the inner exception text
instead of the existing dispatch wrapper; fixed the test, not the refusal.

Canonical/full CI and fresh real-content qualification pending at writing. This
does not assemble automatic installed discovery, prove recipient satisfaction,
complete external/noisy memory/Halo/physical erasure, sustained agenda/outcomes/
reflection, migration/portability or ten fresh complete clean rounds. No production
deployment; keep the full original objective and revise this design from actual
results without adding another independent framework.
