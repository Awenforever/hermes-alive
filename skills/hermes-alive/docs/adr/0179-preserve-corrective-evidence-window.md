# ADR 0179: separate corrective targets from the original evidence window

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: implemented; canonical qualification pending.

## Measured failure

A fresh public-source experiment with faithfully quoted, explicitly authorized
interests reaches complete shadow preparation, but is not accepted for usefulness
or semantic accuracy. A corrective review loses the already-read original paper
page: initial interpretation has seven observations, while its repair sees only
the rejected candidate's search listing. Venue information available initially
therefore becomes unsupported during repair. Search and original-page namespaces
must remain separate; merging them would destroy their independent read scopes.

## Decision

Preserve the initial interpretation's pinned, bounded evidence window for a new
repair. Validate the rejected retained proposal and owner as before. Resolve only
the original observation IDs through existing current-access checks; never query
the latest pool. Deduplicate unions and reject an oversized window or corrective
context rather than silently truncating it. PlanningRuntime separately restricts
repair action targets to rejected candidates. Permission to consult a sibling
source is not permission to introduce its candidate into the selection frame.

No new store, judge, provider, sender or queue is introduced. Existing completed
operations retain their stored source IDs, digests and receipts. Reopening does
not widen a historical repair, rewrite a negative review or spend inference again.

## Evidence and remaining work

Controlled planning tests retain both listing and original page, exclude later
and foreign-owner evidence, reject a sibling action target, and replay without
new events or inference. A read-only copy of actual retained experimental state
confirms seven original versus one historical repair observation; prospective
repair resolves the original seven while all historical completions and reviews
remain valid and no journal event is added. No model call or send is involved.

The complete real experiment's technical success is not product acceptance. Its
final wording remains a release-style inventory and may conflate a named model
with its broader product family's deployment. Review agreement is not ground
truth. Earlier private harness instructions were also incorrectly represented as
user preferences; the new private experiment verifies exact user quotations in
vault, memory and prepared input, without rewriting earlier experiments. That
test repair is not a qualified public memory-intake implementation.

Next audit competing draft/plan representations across factual review, selection,
expression and authorization before changing their contracts. Do not merely hide
a draft from one reviewer while leaving ambiguous downstream consumers. Continue
the bounded useful loop and long-term memory/agenda/feedback, host integration,
migration and ten fresh complete clean-round requirements. Production, Core,
models, other plugins and wmw remain unchanged; no deployment or send is implied.
