# ADR-0287 — Make discovery shares work as conversation openings

The streaming-video sample explained a useful capability through a concrete
demonstration, then continued into training details, model variants and
throughput figures. The opening became longer than the recipient wanted.
The r952 library draft illustrated a related failure: it filled a second
sentence with presumed omissions of the announcement, even though only an
extract was available. Final review caught the evidence error but treated
the extra material as compatible with the recipient's preferences.

Use one authoring instruction for new source-led shares. Lead with something
worth conveying, then explain it with the detail it actually needs. Keep
qualifications that change the point and let ordinary conversation handle
optional depth. An absence claim about a complete source requires evidence
of that scope. The rule is independent of whether a source is technical and
imposes no word count. It does not change the treatment of work outcomes,
requested detail, alerts or time-sensitive communication.

The existing final assessment judges fit and factual support separately.
For new source-led shares, fit now requires a positive reason for the
recipient to hear the message and looks for loss of focus from side details.
A factual but unfocused draft can therefore enter the existing one-revision
path. Facts, source witnesses, search alignment, claim scope and all
delivery checks remain required. The review uses the existing model call.
Existing records continue to use the review scope, prompt and provenance
they were recorded with.

Acceptance needs the outgoing text and real ordinary follow-ups from the
same installed profile. Include simple public discoveries, substantive
technical material that earns a longer explanation, and situations where
silence has more value. Check whether the opening lands, whether the answer
can expand from retained evidence, and whether a short answer can respect
uncertainty without reciting unrelated caveats. Controlled tests qualify
the contract and historical recovery. Real user-facing quality is a
separate gate.

## Real trial after the change

The installed r961 run searched a fresh domestic technical topic, reached
the institutional announcement and the cited paper, then completed one
local submission and two ordinary main-agent follow-ups. The opener led
with a practical distinction about downstream training data. It then
expanded into five paragraphs covering benchmark scores, downstream
results, implementation, OCR and commercial deployment. The new review
prompt was present on the actual request. The reviewer treated topic
interest as evidence for depth and accepted the article-shaped result.
The first follow-up answered in one sentence. The second asserted there
had been no real-robot test and later said the available abstract could
not settle that question.

This round failed. Topic relevance may influence selection, while the
appropriate depth must come from the actual conversational purpose or an
explicit presentation choice. Preserve the paper and additional evidence
for later turns. In a subsequent revision, qualify the decision to include
each major point against the actual opening's purpose. For ordinary
follow-ups, keep claims about what has and has not been demonstrated
bounded to the material actually accessible. Test both failures with fresh
material and the full emitted text before counting a round.

## Separate presentation evidence from selection evidence

Keep topic interests, recall and earlier reactions available to candidate
selection. For new discovery authoring and final review, pass only verified
explicit communication preferences from that context. Existing confirmed
presentation settings still apply in authoring. The author and reviewer
judge secondary detail by what the opening loses if it moves to a follow-up.
An explanation can stay substantial where the context, evidence or explicit
communication choice warrants it. The encrypted evidence and prior prompt
contracts remain intact for replay and deletion. The same filtered review
input is used when a draft needs repair.

Ordinary follow-ups now label both inline and paged evidence as retained
material. A page marked complete can be the entire retained excerpt while
still representing only part of a source. The main turn should answer
questions about missing experiments or comparisons at that scope. An explicit
negative in the evidence can support a negative answer. An omitted passage
calls for an uncertainty statement or a read of available evidence.

The next real trial, r971, confirms the intended distinction in the actual
review request: the explicit communication preference was supplied while
topic interests were absent. The reviewer rejected a multiple-site roundup.
The existing revision produced a two-paragraph share about one site and
connected its archaeological evidence to a concrete idea. The short
follow-up was still a dense sentence. The longer one correctly said that
the retained material could not establish independent validation, but
overstated what the publication omitted. The extractor had timed out and
only search listings were retained. This trial earns no quality credit.
Both inline and paged references now identify search-result excerpts
explicitly. Where no page body was read, the reader must preserve that
limit throughout its answer. Acquisition reliability remains a separate
test of the configured Hermes web tools.

## Make the first answer carry its evidence limit

A new public museum and conservation run reached the institutional article,
produced a share about laser cleaning of paper records and completed two
ordinary Core turns. The opener framed a concrete conservation problem. The
first reply fit one sentence but repeated most of the caveats. The second
opened with a categorical denial of a demonstration and later explained that
retained text could not settle whether a demonstration took place. It also
named internal source indices. The article confirms tests were reported and
participants toured the lab. Its text does not establish what visitors saw
or what a photograph shows. This round fails the continuation requirement.

The main-turn context now asks that the first sentence match the level of
evidence, including unresolved outcomes, rather than deferring a qualification
until after a categorical answer. It asks for human-readable source names.
The scoped read tool retains page and source coverage metadata and identifies
indices as lookup arguments. This changes the instruction reaching the same
Core main turn and keeps the excerpt and lookup budget. A fresh real-model
check must verify the actual opening and source attribution in the response.

## Keep reading notes out of discovery authoring

The transit trial's protected meaning contained only the canonical source link.
Its retained planning goal enumerated several practical topics, but the recorded
`source-led-v3` path already removes that goal from the author request. The
same path still injected a reading assessment with two lengthy quotations and
summaries of station lists, operating hours, transfer rules and the airport
shuttle. The first reviewer found the resulting bulletin factually supported
and unfit for an unsolicited conversation. A retained revision focused on the
free-trial window and the transfer rule, then timed out at re-review.

For new source-led operations, a versioned author-input choice omits the
internal reading assessment. The author still reads the actual excerpts and
the owner's explicit presentation preferences. Selection and audit retain the
reading findings, and factual review and later conversation retain access to
the original evidence. The reader can be thorough without passing an editorial
inventory to the writer. Old receipts reconstruct their original contract.
This removes one demonstrated source of duplication. A real-model round must
still establish that the opening is focused, useful and grounded.

## A cited claim does not ground the rest of the draft

In r1018, the new author input carried one search-result excerpt and no
internal reading inventory. The actual cycle made one search and no page
read. The author wrote four paragraphs about a boreal burned-area dataset.
It added a 1 km MODIS burned-area comparison and an algorithm explanation
that the retained excerpt could not establish. The same review explicitly
recognized the difference between the 1 km active-fire input and the 500 m
burned-area output, then called the comparison supported anyway. The
structural check verified two authentic witness spans about the dataset;
neither span covered the comparison. One local adapter submission passed.

NASA's MCD64A1 user guide identifies the distinct resolutions. The original
research announcement describes a 30 m Landsat comparison, reports the
study's validation metrics and links the dataset directly. The page was
available to this audit. It was never part of the agent's trial evidence.
The July announcement also establishes the result as an older discovery,
despite a search intended to find November releases.

Both ordinary Hermes follow-ups ran. The first gave one dense sentence
listing checks instead of explaining the most useful thing to look for.
The second consistently limited its answer to the search excerpt, but
repeated that limitation and recited extraction metadata. The trial fails
the quality gate and had no physical delivery.

For the next qualification, separate discovery leads from evidence usable
in a share. A successful search provides leads. Candidate selection may
choose to read one, switch to a supported item, or abstain. If the draft
uses a comparison, the evidence must establish the named entities, values
and relation that the text asserts. Authentic evidence for a different
statement cannot cover it, and the reviewing model's background knowledge
cannot replace a missing source. Reproduce the omission with an approving
review before qualifying a correction. Include a supported comparison and
a brief useful opening as positive controls. Keep the same policy for
other subjects and retain the source boundary for subsequent replies.

Primary records: [research announcement](https://www.slrss.cn/kycg/202607/t20260710_849344.html)
and [NASA MCD64A1 user guide](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/s3fs-public/2025-04/MCD64_User_Guide_V61.pdf).

The new review contract preserves older receipts and adds a deterministic
coverage check to the existing review. Each explicit digit-based number in
the actual draft must be inside a verified claim span whose selected passage
contains the number. Citation destinations and list numbering are excluded.
A missing or unrelated witness yields an unresolved finding, so a model's
overall approval cannot authorize the original draft. The existing review
and correction paths still judge scope, attribution and meaning. No
additional model call is required for the coverage check.

This check establishes a minimum evidence link for explicit numbers. A
matching number alone does not establish the unit, population, causal
relationship or product it describes. Nonnumeric claims still require
semantic review, and unsupported conversions need source support or
revision. Search-to-page selection and useful, concise follow-ups remain
separate real-model qualification work.
