# ADR0228 — Evidence must become understanding, not a publisher inventory

Date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Shanghai).
Status: real recipient rejected announcement-like sample; change under qualification.

An actual supported source-led share was factually faithful and passed final
review, yet left the recipient without a usable concept of the change. The
planner already used a version3 editorial brief with no mandatory fact list;
the writer was free to choose. Therefore changing protected-plan rules would
not address this failure. Source approval and topical relevance are insufficient
evidence of a useful conversational contribution.

Align reading choice, bounded citation followup, authorship and the existing
final source/value review on recipient understanding. Seek the concrete result,
demonstrated work, distinction or usable scale supported by the material. Do not
replace publicity wording with invented examples, assumed personal impact or a
benchmark unsupported by accessible evidence. Checkable arithmetic can clarify
a quantity; an announcement does not prove real-world reliability. A simple
interesting fact needs no technical evaluation. This adds no fixed article
template, word quota, required opinion, phrase blacklist or extra model judge.

The existing optional citation-read capability can fill a consequential gap
within its original consent, public-source boundaries and two-resource limit.
Unavailable information remains unknown; it is neither a mandatory critical
paragraph nor permission to invent a result. No new public query or private
profile disclosure follows from this objective.

Keep exact source/literal/authority checks. Existing structural suites establish
protocol boundaries only. Re-evaluate original model text, not a developer's
rewrite; preserve the rejected sample and assess fresh topics separately. A
recipient's sample-specific feedback is not silently promoted into permanent
interest or personality memory. Model routing stays with Hermes.
