# ADR 0071 — Validate source support separately from expression fidelity

Status: architecture direction accepted; source-support admission NOT implemented.

## Measured observation

Three real interpretations of one authored public synthetic discovery used the
configured Hermes main/fallback lane. All actually returned `deepseek-flash` from
`custom:ustc`, with three logical calls and three observed HTTP 200 responses.
Each proposed one SHARE with host-default NONE authority and no schema rejection.

The baseline had explicit repair interest and no learned advice (408 characters).
The next had one weak, evidenced concise-style support signal (280 characters).
The final retained that advice but added an explicit request for detail (607
characters). This is directionally consistent with considering advice and giving
the explicit request priority. Single sequential, non-blinded stochastic samples
are not causal attribution, calibrated adaptation, user satisfaction or benefit.

The baseline copied the internal synthetic source label into its message. That
may be prudent disclosure of this particular fake fixture, not necessarily a
production leak or a false claim. We must distinguish user-facing provenance
from internal collector codes and test metadata, without hiding that evidence is
synthetic or unverified. Do not ban a keyword based on this example.

The detailed candidate added the absolute assurance "nothing is lost since
there's no signup or cost involved." No signup or money does not prove zero time,
attention or other cost. A later renderer could faithfully preserve this original
unsupported draft. Meaning fidelity alone therefore cannot supply source support.

## Direction

Introduce a distinct source-support assessment before treating a generated
proposal as an eligible communication intent. It should compare claims and
recommendations against the actual pinned discovery input, explicit context and
advice roles, not merely compare a rewritten message with the model's own draft.
Role-labeled evidence should separate factual excerpts, human-facing attribution,
host provenance/test metadata, tentative interpretation and action authority.

Support review must permit useful compression and explicitly tentative reasoning;
it must not require pasting every source sentence, invent missing detail to satisfy
a demand for detail, or prohibit all recommendations. It must preserve caveats,
avoid unsupported absolute guarantees and distinguish facts from suggestions.

Bind review to exact source/context epochs, candidate content and actual independent
reviewer identity. Invalid/incomplete findings are unresolved, not an accepted
proposal or proof of successful semantic rejection. Reuse retained paid proposals
for review rather than regenerate a cleaner answer under a fresh output directory.
No new send authority may be inferred from a model verdict.

## Still unproven

No new support-review runtime or admission gate exists at this checkpoint. The
current candidate is tentative and cannot prove its own truth, usefulness, policy
permission or real-world source validity. The broader source-support work and
earlier invalid reviewer calibration case remain open. No production change or
public release follows from this pilot.
