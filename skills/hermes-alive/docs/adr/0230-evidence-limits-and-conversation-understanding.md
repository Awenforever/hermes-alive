# ADR0230 — Collection limits are not negative facts about the world

Date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Shanghai).
Status: structurally qualified; actual main-model overclaim remains unresolved.

Fresh real-source trials establish two distinct product problems. One scientific
share was understandable but did not clearly convey what made its central
relationship interesting. A different technical share explained a concrete
failure mechanism, yet the ordinary main-model followup converted missing
replication evidence in the available material into an assertion that no
independent replication existed. Factual collection and natural continuation
must preserve the scope of knowledge, not merely reproduce the topic.

## Narrow implementation

Keep the authenticated share, sources, collector metadata and delivery state
unchanged. The conversation projection exposes what was actually collected:
the retained excerpt and its reported reading scope and representation. Negative
collector proof flags remain in diagnostics rather than entering dialogue as
apparently global negative facts. Explicitly distinguish an evidence limit from
absence of independent replication or later results. Faithful explanation in
ordinary words is also different from adding an unsupported factual assertion.
This does not establish verification, make sources authoritative, or authorize
new reads, model calls, memory collection or delivery.

Composition uses mathematical notation readable in ordinary chat without
assuming a LaTeX renderer. Values, units, qualifications and protected literals
retain their original obligations. No post-generation formula regex or silent
rewriting is introduced.

## What is not adopted

A controlled four-call comparison on two fixed public materials changed only
the writing task framing. The shorter conversational prompt could still list
scores, exaggerate comparative capability and misstate a model-name change.
That experiment does not justify replacing the production writing prompt or
claiming its length is the root cause. Nor should an agent's internal research
assignment be restored as the recipient's goal to make a story sound purposeful.

The point worth sharing should become apparent through a supported explanation,
relationship, distinction or possibility. It need not become a required benefits
paragraph, application claim, technical breakthrough, personal feeling or
criticism. No topic-specific style blacklist or fixed article template follows.

## Separate memory work

An offline actual-Core probe with synthetic messages found that
`SessionStore.load_transcript` requests durable replay repair; it is not a pure
advisory read. `SessionDB.get_messages` supports an exact-session bounded tail,
but its row bound does not bound body size, and a `user` role can also contain
generated notifications and quoted text. Therefore no private-history reader is
enabled by this change. Curated-profile consent must not silently expand into
dialogue processing or permanent preference learning. The native feedback path
still needs an explicit, scoped product integration; component APIs alone do not
establish ordinary daily learning.

## Verification boundary

Check actual Core context injection, exact original share, preserved diagnostic
records, erased/foreign/session-reset refusal and model-visible evidence scope.
Replay the original public continuation through the actual main model before
claiming behavioral improvement. Deterministic projection tests prove scope
handling, not that a model cannot overclaim. Real content usefulness, ten fresh
full-chain rounds, fleet lifecycle and production acceptance remain separate.

## Qualification update, 2026-10-25

The actual supported-Core continuity/setup/control/authoring focus passes, as
does the complete98 portable suite. Two actual-model notation cases produce
plain-text arithmetic for semantic content while preserving an explicitly
protected LaTeX literal exactly. These checks do not establish article quality.

A fixed-share before/after main-model comparison still fails semantically. The
new response correctly says missing material is not proof of nonexistence, then
ends with a stronger negative conclusion not justified by that material. Keep
the clearer evidence projection, but do not report this as a complete repair or
promote the correct disclaimer into proof that the whole reply is faithful.
