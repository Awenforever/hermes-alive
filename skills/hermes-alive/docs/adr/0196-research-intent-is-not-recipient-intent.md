# ADR0196 — Internal research intent is not recipient intent

Status: implemented boundary; qualification pending

## Evidence and decision

The source-led real trial completed generation, selection, actual authorship,
semantic assessment, final source/value assessment and no-inference reopen. Its
v3 plan no longer imposed a fact inventory, but the final contribution remained
a dataset summary with unsolicited methodology advice and collection caveats.
Model-supported is not recipient-accepted; no quality round was credited.

One bounded input-only contrast removed the generating model's recipient_goal,
behavior_constraints and tentative reading tasks from the same author's input.
The unsolicited research assignment disappeared, but source compression remained.
This single stochastic contrast neither proves causality nor solves quality.
It does reveal an avoidable responsibility leak: the writer and final assessor
were given an agent-invented assignment alongside actual recipient context.

For new source-led-v2 discovery operations, keep research questions and value
hypotheses in the encrypted research/selection history, not the authorship or
semantic-review inputs. Keep the generator's editorial plan out of final
source/value assessment as well. The author gets original source scope, actual
explicit owner context, presentation and immutable required link/speech boundary;
the final assessor judges actual text, evidence and recipient context, not
whether the generator's own proposed plan was accomplished.

No new judge, topic-specific rule, phrase blacklist, length quota, source,
provider, model policy, memory store or transport queue is introduced. Existing
prompts and review schemas are reused. Source/currentness/privacy/expiry/owner
context validation still resolves the original proposal and bound inquiry; the
change is input-role separation, not deletion of provenance or authority checks.
Strict v1/v2 requests/commitments/reminders/warnings are unchanged.

## History and tests

The recorded synthesis discovery_authoring mode, matched against its encrypted
bundle, chooses the input projection. Historical source-led-v1 operations retain
their original author context and final snapshot exactly, including semantic
protocol recovery. New v2 uses a distinct reconstructed context/final snapshot
mode; old approval/review hashes cannot be reinterpreted as new proof. Structural
proposal admission remains proposal-shape-v3: its accepted shape has not changed.

Actual encrypted integration retains private marker goals, constraints, inquiry
questions and value hypotheses in provenance and selection, but asserts absence
from new author/semantic/final inputs. It exercises structural and semantic
protocol correction, current final witnesses, missing/negative-witness refusal,
source withdrawal during authoring, historical-v1 reopen under v2 defaults and
no-inference completion replay. These are boundary tests, not quality rounds.

Qualification must use actual terminal results. This does not claim to solve
source novelty/depth, natural voice, autonomous installed discovery, persistent
memory/agenda/outcomes/reflection, Halo/erasure, migration/portability, ten fresh
complete clean rounds or production acceptance. Next prioritize an integrated
installed discovery cycle with useful fresh outcomes, not more variations of the
same dataset paragraph. Production remains unchanged.
